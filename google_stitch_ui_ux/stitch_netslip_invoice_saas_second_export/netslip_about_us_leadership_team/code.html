01 • THE INCIDENT Lagos, November 2023

The ₦1.8M phantom payment that shuttered an artisan atelier.

It began on a frantic Friday evening in a bespoke bridal boutique in Lekki Phase 1. The proprietor dispatched three hand-beaded lace dresses after receiving a pristine bank transfer screenshot inside WhatsApp. Three days later, her balance remained unchanged. The alert had been assembled via an underground pixel-manipulation bot. By dawn Monday, four neighboring boutique owners reported identical losses totaling millions.

02 • THE COGNITIVE DISSONANCE Design Principle

Why enterprise ERPs will never conquer conversational commerce.

Traditional enterprise software assumes a merchant sits at an ergonomic desk with high-bandwidth broadband and an accounting degree. Africa’s commerce vitality thrives on moving motorcycles, noisy textile markets, and frantic voice notes. Forcing a merchant to abandon their native chat thread to navigate clunky multi-step accounting portals is fundamentally flawed. Invoicing must take place where the dialogue happens.

03 • THE SLIP PHILOSOPHY Tactile Ledger

Bringing physical permanence into digital air.

We spent hundreds of hours analyzing vintage physical sales books across Balogun Market and Kantamanto. The perforated tear, the inked stamp, the handwritten signature of gratitude—these were not decorative trivialities. They were legal and social tokens of honor. NetSlip preserves that tactile sanctity through rigorous typographic hierarchy, micro-perforations, and unshakeable cryptographic backing.
