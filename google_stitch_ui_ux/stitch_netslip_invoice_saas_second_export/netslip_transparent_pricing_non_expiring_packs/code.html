Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers, No Hidden Fine Print

Do non-expiring credit packs really never expire?

Yes. If you buy a 50 or 150-pack today and only issue 10 slips this month, the remaining balance stays live in your merchant wallet for months or years. We will never zero out purchased packs due to inactivity.

How do local payment methods work?

You can fund your account or pay subscriptions directly through Nigerian bank transfer, USSD, debit cards via Paystack/Flutterwave, or multi-currency channels. No USD card required.

Can I alternate between subscription and PAYG packs?

Absolutely. Many seasonal sellers subscribe to Pro Merchant during November and December for unlimited holiday throughput, and revert to a non-expiring PAYG pack for the remainder of the year.

What happens when a buyer clicks on my NetSlip link?

Buyers receive a tamper-proof cryptographic receipt page showing transaction timestamps, payment confirmation, your contact details, and itemized summaries. They can download it as a thermal print PDF or save it to their phone wallet without creating an account.
